# Lab title

## Status
Planned / In progress / Completed with evidence

## Business request
Describe the request, scope, and approved outcome.

## Environment and prerequisites
Record roles, licenses, tools, and lab assumptions.

## Initial state
Record users, groups, and relevant access before changing anything.

## Procedure
Number each action and explain its purpose.

## Verification
Compare expected and actual results; link redacted evidence.

## Troubleshooting
Record symptoms, investigation, cause, fix, and retest. Do not invent incidents.

## Lessons learned
Explain what worked and what you would improve.

## Cleanup / rollback
Record the restoration plan and actual cleanup performed.
